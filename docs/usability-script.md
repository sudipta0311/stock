# Usability script

Run each task on a 360 × 740 viewport with a fresh test household. Use synthetic data and a prepared statement file. Record completion time and any errors.

| Task | Target |
|---|---:|
| Add a fixed deposit with a bank, amount, rate, and maturity date. | Under 30 seconds |
| Upload a CAS statement and reach its review screen. | Under 2 minutes |
| Find what to do this week from Home. | Under 10 seconds |
| Find the safe money target from Plan. | Under 15 seconds |
| Add a spouse and set their tax residence. | Under 60 seconds |

## Observations to record

- Whether the person finds the main action without help.
- Time to complete, including any pauses or backtracking.
- Labels or instructions the person asks about.
- Errors, uncertainty, or information they expected to see.
- Whether optional details stay out of the way until requested.
- The first action they take after reaching the plan.
