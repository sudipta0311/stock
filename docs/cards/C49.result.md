# C49 result

## Summary

- Added weekly, point-in-time stock checks for screened and directly held shares. Rows contain public facts only, retain 12 weeks, and run in an isolated weekly job.
- Added deterministic holding reviews for fresh IN and US checks, including revenue, governance, valuation, price risk, position size, tax notes, and shared buy room.
- Prioritized eligible held shares for add-more steps, kept the single weekly sell action, and added a Money highlight for other holdings that need attention.
- Replaced the raw exit card with the plain-language “Our view” card, closed check details, Money labels, and a Home-to-Money highlight.

| Verdict | Facts that drive it | Copy shown |
|---|---|---|
| Sell | A fresh check leads the exit rules to an exit, such as two consecutive revenue declines or a critical company event. | “Sell”; “Company revenue fell for {count} quarters in a row.” or “A recent company event needs a closer look.” |
| Think about selling | A fresh check leads to a review, such as a price risk with a revenue warning or a material company event. | “Think about selling”; “The price is down {drawdown_pct}% from your recorded cost.” or “A recent event may affect this company.” |
| Sell some | A fresh check triggers a valuation or position-size reduction. A tax wait can preserve the suggested timing. | “Sell some”; “The price looks high against its own history.” or “This share is {share_of_equity_pct}% of your stock investments.” |
| Add more | No check is triggered or watched; the rank is at least 8 of 10; the own-history P/E rank is below 80; and shared buy room meets the market minimum. | “Add more”; “Recent checks support adding more shares.” |
| Keep | The check is fresh, no higher-priority sell or review applies, and the add-more conditions are not all met. | “Keep”; a plain reason appears only when a check needs context. |
| Not checked | No fresh check exists within 14 days, or the share is outside IN and US. Only price history and position size are checked; no sell or add-more action is created. | “We don't follow this company's results yet, so we only check its price and size.” |

Shared card copy includes “Our view”, “Checked on {date}”, and “How we check”. Check details start closed. Plain status labels are “Looks okay”, “Worth a look”, “Needs attention”, and “Not enough information”.

Tax lines show the Indian estimate or waiting date where available. A share savings account says “In your share savings account, tax is the same whether you sell or keep.” Other unmodeled accounts say “Tax is not estimated for this account.”
