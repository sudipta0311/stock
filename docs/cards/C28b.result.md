## Summary
- Added shared visual tokens for typography, spacing, card surfaces, radii, colors, and shadows across light and dark themes.
- Refined the app shell, bottom navigation, buttons, notes, form controls, empty states, loading skeletons, and Money/Ideas rows for phone layouts.

## Screen-by-screen
- **App shell:** 56 px header with the logo mark, page title, and profile button; household selectors sit below it. Removed role chips from the header. Bottom tabs have a soft active icon pill and safe-area padding.
- **Home:** Net worth is now a brand-tinted hero with a larger tabular amount, rounded secondary currency, and an “as of” line. Weekly actions use compact rows. The no-action state has a success icon and one review button.
- **Plan:** The allocation bar is 12 px high with 2 px segment gaps. With no assets, the screen shows “Add what you have to see your plan” and a button to Money instead of a zero bar or safe-money summary.
- **Money:** Upload remains first. Asset and holding rows use 36 px icon tiles, muted sub-lines, aligned tabular amounts, and chevrons. With no assets, a one-sentence empty state and button take users to the checklist.
- **Ideas:** Fund and idea rows use 36 px icon tiles, clearer title and sub-line grouping, and aligned amounts where present.
- **Forms:** Inputs are at least 48 px high. Amount inputs show their currency prefix and tabular numbers; the formatted rupee amount remains below the field. The rough-number helper now appears once below the form title, with hints kept only where useful.
- **Onboarding:** Progress and step labels are quieter, back links align with the card content and name the previous step, and the final step has no back link. Removed the duplicate “You” label above Tax residence.
- **Settings:** The household role appears only in Settings and only when another login is present. Currency prefixes were added to income and investing amount fields.
- **Shared states:** Highlights have severity icons, a three-item limit, and matching “Why?” and “Hide” tap targets. Loading placeholders follow card shapes. Cards and controls use consistent surfaces, spacing, and corner radii.
