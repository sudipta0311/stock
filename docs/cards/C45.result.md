## Recorded-buy readers and double-counting
- **Money and net-worth snapshots:** the Money API and snapshot loader read waiting buys. Known securities join their existing holding group; unknown-security buys count at recorded cost in their sleeve. Once matched, the temporary buy stops contributing and the imported holding is the single source.
- **Policy holdings and digest:** the policy loader includes waiting buys in holdings, grouping known securities with existing positions so stock, sector, satellite, and US-situs checks see them once. Unknown-security buys use cost in their target sleeve. Recorded buys are in the digest so edits and removals rebuild the plan.
- **This-week spending:** linked waiting or matched buys reduce the member's new money for that week. A completed, eligible spending action without a recorded buy reduces it by the action amount. Linked buys are not also charged through the completed-action fallback.
- **Share-savings room:** waiting buys reduce this year's room unless that member has an imported cash flow dated on or after the buy; that flow already accounts for the purchase.
- **Fund checks and tax lots:** waiting mutual-fund units enter the existing checks and lot path. Confirmation marks an eligible recorded buy matched in the same transaction, so the imported fund position replaces its temporary valuation.
- **Statement matching and import review:** confirmation reads waiting buys for that import's member only. Security and date rules, or the configured settlement window for security-less CAS buys, determine matching. Matched buys stop counting before the confirmation response is returned.
- **Home, Money, and Settings:** Home reads the action's recorded-buy flag, Money reads waiting-buy details and totals, and Settings export includes the records. These views do not create extra holdings; all values come from the same waiting records and API responses.

## Review round 4
- The INR 1,500 repeat came from reducing new money before the weekly split. The full INR 12,345.60 plan allocated INR 2,000 to its DKK 162 line and carried INR 10,345.60. DKK 162 converted to INR 1,999.99 on the recorded date, so the full-line rule charges the planned INR 2,000. Subtracting it before allocation made policy split INR 10,345.60 again, creating a new INR 1,500 buy and reducing carry to INR 8,845.60. The gap came from reallocation after the split, not DKK rounding, an account quantum, omitted lines, or a mismatch between the step and its line.

Absent-record done buy steps still spend their step amount.
- Abroad lines keep DKK and INR amounts in sync when reduced. Remainders below the INR action minimum are carried forward. Optional approved share-pick lines also respect the existing DKK 2,000 minimum; fund lines use the INR action minimum.
