## Prices

The refresh uses [Alpha Vantage daily time series](https://www.alphavantage.co/documentation/) and stores source/date with each quote. The worker reserves 11 daily calls for the curated funds and allows up to 13 held US shares, for a maximum of 24 calls. Alpha Vantage documents a free allowance of 25 requests per day on its [support page](https://www.alphavantage.co/support/). Its [terms](https://www.alphavantage.co/terms_of_service/) describe personal, non-commercial use; broader use needs provider approval. The `marketdata-abroad` workflow exits successfully with a notice when the key is absent. No test calls the network.
