## Decisions / open issues

- `loan_events` has no per-user natural unique key beyond its ID, so no duplicate rule was needed in `app_merge_into_household`.
- Danish interest relief remains review-only. SKAT confirms qualifying interest expenses, but no single relief percentage is applied because the effective amount depends on the household's tax details.
- Tax-relief rule data and sources (`verified: false` for each):
  - `in_home_self_occupied.old`: interest cap INR 200,000; `https://www.incometax.gov.in/iec/foportal/help/individual/return-applicable-1?mobile-app=1`; `verified: false`.
  - `in_home_self_occupied.new`: not eligible; `https://www.incometax.gov.in/iec/foportal/help/new-tax-vs-old-tax-regime-faqs?mobile-app=1`; `verified: false`.
  - `in_home_let_out.old`: no numeric interest cap; loss set-off cap INR 200,000; `https://www.incometax.gov.in/iec/foportal/help/individual/return-applicable-1?mobile-app=1`; `verified: false`.
  - `in_home_let_out.new`: no numeric interest cap; no set-off against other income heads; `https://www.incometax.gov.in/iec/foportal/help/individual/return-applicable-1?mobile-app=1`; `verified: false`.
  - `in_education.old`: no numeric cap encoded; `https://www.incometax.gov.in/iec/foportal/help/individual/return-applicable-1`; `verified: false`.
  - `in_education.new`: not eligible; `https://www.incometax.gov.in/iec/foportal/help/new-tax-vs-old-tax-regime-faqs?mobile-app=1`; `verified: false`.
  - `dk_interest_deduction`: qualifying Danish interest expenses only; no cap or fixed deduction rate encoded; `https://skat.dk/en-us/borger/fradrag/fradrag-for-renter`; `verified: false`.
- Worked example: for a ₹30 L home loan at 8.5% with 240 months remaining and a ₹2 L extra payment, keeping the ₹26,034.70 monthly payment finishes 36 months sooner and saves ₹7.57 L interest. Keeping the end date lowers the payment to ₹24,299.05 and saves ₹2.17 L interest.
- Full pytest remains blocked only by access to the default Windows temp directory. Resolving it would require changing that external directory's permissions or changing the requested pytest temp configuration.
