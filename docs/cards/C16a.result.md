## Decisions / open issues
- NSE daily archive URL: `https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/content/indices/ind_close_all_{DDMMYYYY}.csv`. The CSV columns used are `Index Name`, `Index Date`, `P/E`, `P/B`, and `Div Yield`. No historical backfill was attempted; the optional archive reliability check was not met.
- Feed records store titles, summaries, GUIDs, and links only. Text caps are 1,024 bytes for titles and summaries, 512 bytes for GUIDs, and 2,048 bytes for links. Linked pages are not fetched. Records are pruned after 120 days.
- At 110 feed items per day for 120 days, the text caps imply about 61 MB of raw text and links; allowing for row and index overhead, estimate under 90 MB. Five years of four index series and 400 days of two Treasury series should add only a few MB, well below the Neon free-tier 0.5 GB allowance.
- The eight full-suite errors are environment-only: pytest could not scan or create its temp lock under the Windows temp root. An alternate OS temp root was also denied; the full suite was run with its requested default basetemp.
