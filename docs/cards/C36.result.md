# C36 result

## Summary

- Home puts this week’s steps first, asks for a monthly amount when needed, and links to the money total.
- Ideas shows the top three ideas per market by default, with buy guidance and the rest behind “See all ideas”.
- Money has one “Add or update” action, a shorter asset list, and closed sections for less common tasks.
- Settings groups existing controls into four closed sections with current-state summaries.
- Highlights now appear on Home only, and server wake-up uses a small updating chip with quiet sign-in retries.

## Screen notes (before / after)

- **Home:** Before, weekly ideas and the investing amount were hard to find. After, this week’s steps lead, the monthly amount is editable there, and the money total links to Money.
- **Ideas:** Before, research was hidden below an empty Denmark card and repeated notes. After, ideas are open by default, Denmark’s empty state follows them, and notes are absent.
- **Money:** Before, the page was long and repeated notes. After, the total and asset rows lead, “Add or update” opens the three existing choices, and extra sections start closed.
- **Plan:** Before, backend highlights could repeat on the plan screen. After, highlights are Home-only; plan placement details remain behind their existing closed “Why this?” section.
- **Settings:** Before, everyday choices shared a long page with many monthly fund rows and rare privacy controls. After, four closed groups summarize their current state; monthly fund amounts are in Money.
- **Sign-in after a wake timeout:** Before, the sign-in page stayed in an error state after the API was unreachable. After, it retries quietly every 20 seconds and returns to the app when the saved session refreshes.
